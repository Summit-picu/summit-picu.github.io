picu Architecture

picu is the central package management component of Summit Linux.

Components 

picu CLI
   │
   ├── /etc/picu/picu.conf
   │
   ├── /etc/picu/mirrors.d/
   │
   ├── /etc/picu/trust/
   │       └── root.json
   │
   ├── /var/picu/mirrors/
   │
   ├── /var/picu/db/
   │
   └── /var/picu/cache/
           │
           ├── sources
           └── binary packages

Directory hierarchy 

/etc/picu/
├── picu.conf
├── mirrors.d/
└── trust/
    └── root.json

/var/picu/
├── mirrors/
├── db/
└── cache/

Mirror workflow

  1. picu downloads the mirror manifest.
  2. The signature is checked.
  3. The signer is compared against the trust store.
  4. CRL status is checked.
  5. Anti-rollback validation is performed.
  6. The mirror becomes available to package operations.
